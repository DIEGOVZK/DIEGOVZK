## 🔭 About me

I design **hardware**, write **firmware**, do **CAD** and computer **simulations**: from satellites to IoT devices. From 2020 to 2025 at Inatel (National Institute of Telecommunications, Brazil), I led CubeSat teams, built satellite subsystems and other robotic and telemetry network projects.

I'm a Computer Engineer (Inatel, 2025) and a research student at Tohoku University, Sendai, Japan. My work is published in IEEE journals, JATM and SBrT.

Teaching runs alongside the engineering: lab classes on microcontrollers and computer networks, plus Inatel short courses on small-satellite development (2022–2023) and hands-on LoRa networks (2025). Beyond hardware I work across the stack: Node.js/TypeScript, databases, Docker, CI/CD, automated tests. I run projects with Agile methods.

🌎 From Minas Gerais, Brazil · 🗣 Portuguese (native) · English (C1, TOEFL iBT 110)
